Space Salvage – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Space Salvage ("the game") is a single-player game for Android published by DEP Properties LLC (Devante Johnson) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

The short version

  • We do not run any servers, accounts, analytics or tracking of our own. We never receive your personal information.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (credits, items, ships, settings and which purchases you own) is saved in a file inside the game's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game or using Settings → Reset all progress deletes it. Android device backups are disabled for this game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Watching an ad is always optional. You choose when to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus, and everything in the game can be done without ads.

When an ad is requested, Google's Mobile Ads SDK (part of AdMob) may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, and the app's identifiers
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and app performance and diagnostic information (for example, crash and timing data)

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. It is sent encrypted. It is handled under Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent. In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options. If you decline, you can still play the whole game, and Google may serve non-personalized or limited ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (the Ad-Free Pass and the Elite Hull Paints) are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment and payment information under the Google Payments Privacy Notice (https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice). The game only receives confirmation from Google Play of which items your Google account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files, precise location, messages or any other personal information. The game has no chat, user accounts or social features. We do not sell any data.

Children

Space Salvage is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated PG or lower.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
